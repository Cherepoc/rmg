### Fix loops: the same issue fixed back and forth

1. **The analytics file's size (closed 11 October 2026: `auto_vacuum = INCREMENTAL`, freed a day at a time after each prune, the journal cut).** On 9 October the file never shrank after a prune (`EventStore.cs:264`), and package 8 (f1ca0bf) added a daily `VACUUM`. On 10 October that `VACUUM` was found running under the write lock on a request thread, leaving a -wal file as large as the database (#363), and package 23 (86610bf) removed it. The high-water mark of 9 October is back. A way out of the loop: `PRAGMA auto_vacuum = INCREMENTAL` with `incremental_vacuum` after each prune and a `wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)`, so that the file shrinks a little at a time without a full rewrite under the lock.
2. **Who a visitor is, for the caps (closed 11 October 2026: a network's ratings and verdicts counted apart from its telemetry).** On 9 October one client posing as new visitors could fill the disk, and the fix was told not to key the cap on the address alone, as people behind one NAT would lose their ratings (the review's angle-contract#2); package 1 added a global daily cap instead. On 10 October one client varying its User-Agent could spend that global cap for everyone (#357), and package 23 added a cap per network, an IPv4 address or an IPv6 /64, of eight visitors' worth a day: keyed on the address after all. An office, a school or a carrier-grade NAT with more than eight active listeners a day now loses events, ratings and verdicts among them. Before a third turn: count ratings and verdicts apart from the network cap, as the per-visitor cap already counts them apart, or raise the network's share.
3. **A drum bound to a polymeter's kick (checked, no loop).** Package 7 of 9 October (eeb1341) kept such a drum playing only where the kick does; package 21 (4935e95) removed that guard so that an accenting drum is never silent through a section. The guard's intent still holds: over three corpora, 148 bound notes in polymeter sections, none off the kick.
4. **The trading solo (narrowed, no loop).** Package 5 of 9 October (5a28b1f) sent every accompanying soloist back to its own part between turns; package 9 (b5e270d) made the counter-melody rest there as the lead does. A narrowing, not a reversal.
5. **Within this review.** Package 18 (8f07ae1) made the doubling test skip polymeter sections; package 21 (4935e95) took the skip out again once it fixed the polymeter itself. And package 26 narrowed the doubling test's fill window from four beats to one bar, which 9 October's eeb1341 had widened; it passes on all three corpora, but is the place to look if a fill ever spans two bars again.
