A roll takes an instrument at random from this range, which leaves out the sound effects General MIDI ends with, as the generator does. Choosing one by hand is not bound by it, and the drums are always rolled from their kits.

RMG is MIT. Playback is spessasynth (Apache-2.0) and .mp3 export is lamejs (LGPL-3.0), both loaded unmodified from a CDN and neither linked against nor derived from.

Soundfonts carry their own licences and are none of this project’s: the one to start with is MuseScore General by S. Christian Collins, after FluidR3 by Frank Wen, which is MIT so long as its acknowledgements and copyright notices travel with it. Every soundfont this server offers links to its own notice above.

This page counts how long things took and how far people got, so the slow parts can be found. It goes to this server and nowhere else, sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser; your address is turned into a number that changes every day and is never written down. The soundfont you choose is counted by its size, never by its name. Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track and none of it is sent at all.
